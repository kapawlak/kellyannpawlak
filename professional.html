The lab coat

I make quantum computers useful.

I'm a theoretical physicist working in quantum computing — currently a Technical Solutions Executive for US Government at Quantinuum. My thing is the bridge between deep physics and real-world problems: novel algorithms, real hardware, actual applications.

The professional headshot. Yes, I clean up nicely.

What I do

Over a decade of quantum research, from theoretical condensed matter physics to shipping real products. My specialty is transforming highly technical results into accessible deliverables — a partner-focused solution, a scientific report, or software for a government agency — and leading the teams that build them, on time and under budget.

My research roots are in theoretical condensed matter physics — strongly correlated systems, the kind of math that makes most people quietly leave the room.

I also coach — I like helping people do hard, scary things deliberately, whether that's a physics problem or a cliff face.
