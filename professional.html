The lab coat

I make quantum computers useful.

I'm a theoretical physicist working in quantum computing — currently at Quantinuum, where I lead government partnerships and projects. My thing is the bridge between deep physics and real-world problems: novel algorithms, real hardware, actual applications.

The professional headshot. Yes, I clean up nicely.

What I do

Five years in quantum computing, split between inventing algorithms and getting them to run on real machines — then helping other people do the same. These days that means working with government partners on problems worth a quantum computer.

My research roots are in theoretical condensed matter physics — strongly correlated systems, the kind of math that makes most people quietly leave the room.

I also coach — I like helping people do hard, scary things deliberately, whether that's a physics problem or a cliff face.
